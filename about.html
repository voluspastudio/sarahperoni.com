about // bio

Sarah Peroni

FILE 01 SP / DESIGN

I'm a multidisciplinary designer working across brand identity, digital interfaces, illustration, and creative direction. The through line is obsessive attention to how things hold together — visually, conceptually, and at every scale.

My work moves between systems and stories. One week it's a brand voice, type system, and full visual identity for a small studio. The next it's a music ezine, a journaling app, or a printed object. I treat every project like a small world: build the rules, build the language, then design what lives inside.

Before going independent I worked in agency and in-house studios on identity, packaging, and digital products. Now I run my own practice from Costa Mesa, California, taking on a small number of long-form projects each year — ones I can sit with, sketch through, and ship in full.

I care about type, color, and weird little details. I draw a lot. I prototype a lot. I think the best brands feel like places you can actually go.
